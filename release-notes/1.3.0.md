## What's New in HealthAnalyzer 1.3.0

### Training Plan (new Plan tab)
- **Training calendar**: A month calendar of your planned workouts and recorded runs. Each day shows a coloured chip by workout type (easy, long, tempo, intervals, recovery, race, cross-training) with its distance, a ✓ with the km you actually ran, and faded chips for missed sessions.
- **AI-written running plans**: Tap **Create plan**, describe your goal and constraints, and pick a length (1 week by default, up to 16 weeks), runs per week and AI model. Your coach builds the plan from your profile, baseline, 12-month stats, weekly totals and recent runs, with target paces and heart rates for every session. A new plan replaces upcoming workouts only; past days stay as history.
- **Day and week detail**: Tap any day to see the workout's targets and instructions next to the runs you recorded, plus the week's planned vs done km.
- **Today's recap**: A card at the top of the Plan tab with today's workout, the local weather forecast and a short AI coach briefing on when to run, what to wear and how to adjust for heat, cold, wind or rain. Turn it on or off and set your city in **Settings → Training Plan**. The forecast comes from Open-Meteo.
- Planned workouts are included in data backups.

### In-App Updates
- The app checks GitHub releases for new versions and can download, verify and install the update for you.

### More Reliable AI Requests
- Analyses, chats and plans keep running when the app goes to the background.
- Requests that drop before the first reply are retried automatically, and long "thinking" pauses no longer time out.
- **Thinking level** per AI provider (Default, Off, Low, Medium, High) for models with extended reasoning.

### Safety
- Deleting an analysis, a conversation or clearing all history now asks for confirmation.
