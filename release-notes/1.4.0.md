## What's New in HealthAnalyzer 1.4.0

### Recovery data for your coach
- **Sleep, energy score and resting heart rate**: The app now syncs each day's sleep score, time asleep, deep/REM/awake time, bedtime and wake time, Samsung's energy score, and a daily resting heart rate. The next sync asks Samsung Health once for permission to read them.
- The training review gets a per-day recovery table and compares the last 7 days with the 4 weeks before. A run analysis gets that day's sleep, energy score and resting HR next to your 4-week average.
- Plan creation, plan changes and Today's recap all see your recent recovery, so a session can be eased after a clearly bad night.
- Use the new `{{recovery}}` placeholder in your own prompts.

### Other training counts too
- **Strength sessions, hikes, bike rides and walks** recorded in Samsung Health (including workouts from apps such as Hevy) are now synced. Sessions under 15 minutes are left out of the prompts.
- Weekly totals show other sessions and their time, and the weekly heart-rate-zone minutes and load now cover all training, not only runs.
- Plan vs actual shows these sessions on the day they happened.
- The first sync after updating re-reads about four months of exercise once, so it takes longer than usual.

### Richer run analysis
- **Per-kilometre splits** now include average and max heart rate, cadence, step length, power, climb and descent, and time paused.
- **Pauses** are listed with where they happened, how long they lasted and your heart rate before and after.
- First half vs second half is compared on moving time.

### Deeper training review
- **Weekly minutes per heart-rate zone and a training load figure** for each week.
- **Runs and treadmill walks are counted apart**, so slow walks no longer distort your running pace and volume.
- **Plan vs actual**: your saved plan next to what you recorded, day by day, with the new `{{plan_review}}` placeholder.
- Each recent run shows its paused time and the rest days before it.

### Training plan
- **Plans cover whole weeks, Monday to Sunday**, starting next Monday. Tick **Also plan the rest of this week** to include the days left in the current week.
- **Change the plan with AI**: tell your coach what came up ("I have a dinner on Friday, move that run") and the upcoming sessions are adjusted. You can undo the last change.

### Planning advice in analyses is now a choice
- A new switch in **Settings → Training Plan → Planning advice in analyses** decides whether run analyses and training reviews end with suggestions for your coming sessions. It is off by default, so analyses review and the Plan tab plans.
- It works through the new `{{planning}}` placeholder. The built-in prompts you already have are updated to use it; text you wrote yourself is left alone.

### Good to know
- Recovery data and other workouts are not part of data backups. A sync brings them back.
