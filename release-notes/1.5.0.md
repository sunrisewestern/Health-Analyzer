## What's New in HealthAnalyzer 1.5.0

### Automatic backup
- **Back up on a schedule** to a folder on your phone, to your WebDAV server, or both. Switch it on in **Settings → Backup & Cloud Restore**, under **File on this phone** (where you also choose the folder) or **WebDAV**.
- Choose how often (every 6 or 12 hours, daily, every 3 days, weekly) and how many automatic backups to keep. Older automatic backups are removed; backups you make by hand are never touched.
- Set a backup password to encrypt automatic backups. With a password you can also include settings & API keys; without one, only running data and AI history are backed up.
- Each destination shows when it last backed up and whether it worked. A problem with one destination doesn't stop the other. **Back up now** runs a backup straight away.

### Thinking levels that match your model
- New levels: **Minimal**, **Extra High** and **Max**, next to Off, Low, Medium and High.
- The Thinking Level picker now offers only what the chosen model accepts. For example, Claude Opus 5.5 offers Low to Max, Gemini 4 offers Low, Medium and High, and GPT-5.2 adds Extra High.
- A saved level the model doesn't accept is replaced by the nearest one it does, so switching models no longer causes failed requests.
- Claude 4.6 and later now use adaptive thinking with an effort level, as Anthropic requires for those models.

### Fixes
- Long model lists in **Fetch Available Models** now scroll, so the last models can be picked.
