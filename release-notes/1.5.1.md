## What's New in Run Analyzer 1.5.1

HealthAnalyzer is now called **Run Analyzer**.

### Models per task
- Use a different AI model and thinking level for each job: **Run analysis**, **Chat**, **Training plan** and **Today's briefing**. For example, a fast model for the daily briefing and a deep-thinking one for plans.
- Set it up in **Settings → AI & Prompts → Providers**, under **Models per task**. For each task, pick a provider, then type a model ID or tap **Fetch models** to pick one from that provider's list. Blank fields use the provider's own settings.
- Pickers and chat headers show the model and thinking level that will actually be sent. A provider you pick on a screen still wins for that request.

### Training plans
- New plans default to **4 weeks**.
- Your saved plan (plan vs recorded and upcoming sessions) can go into analyses (switch on the Analyze tab) and chats (per-chat toggle).

### Health Connect
- **Health Connect** (read only) is a new data source: runs with heart rate, speed, cadence, power and route, plus other workouts, sleep and resting heart rate. Connect it in **Settings → Data & Integrations**.
- Workouts that also come from Samsung Health or Garmin are taken from those, so nothing is counted twice.

### Automatic sync
- Samsung Health, Garmin and Health Connect sync in the background on a schedule (daily by default), when the app opens, and before today's briefing is written. Settings and the last sync status are under **Data & Integrations**.
- Today's briefing is rewritten when a run is recorded today. The Today card is always shown; its setting now only controls whether the briefing is written automatically.

### Chat
- Attach photos and videos to chat messages.

### Other changes
- WebDAV backups now go into a **RunAnalyzer** folder inside your WebDAV URL, created automatically. Restore still lists backups in the old place.
- New adaptive launcher icon.
